04Regions & Activities

Bishkek (Pre-Trip)

FRI 26 – SUN 28 SEP · 2 NIGHTS · 800M ALTITUDE
Stay
Freedom Hotel, Isanov St 144. 4.9★. ~USD 60–80/night. Central, walkable.
Do
Sat: Lenin Square walk, Parliament, State Museum (Soviet grandeur). Shoro Bazaar locals' market (early 7–9am, no tourists, haggle). Café culture. Sun: Market wander, Chu River evening walk, rest before operator pickup.
Eat
Plov House (traditional). Hotel breakfast included. Shoro street food (kebabs, lagman). Modern coffee shops (Bishkek café scene).

Karakol (Basecamp)

SUN 28 SEP – WED 1 OCT · 3 NIGHTS · 1,600M ALTITUDE
Stay
Central Guest House Karakol, Boronbay Khana St 36. 5.0★. ~USD 35–50/night. Family-run, can arrange all guides, meals.
Do (Pick 2–3)
① Eagle hunting/falconry center (morning, ~USD 50–80). ② Skazka Canyon (Fairytale Canyon, 2–3hr, geology/photo). ③ Mars Canyon or Canyon of Forgotten Rivers (hidden, 4–5hr, Visit Karakol guides). ④ Kaindy Valley petroglyphs (remote, explorer feel). ⑤ Thermal springs soak (Altyn Arashan or Jyrgalan, 45min).
Eat
Guesthouse home-cooked meals. Kumys (fermented mare's milk, acquired taste). Bishbarmak (meat + noodles, eaten by hand). Local market fresh bread & yogurt.

Song-Kul Plateau (Centerpiece)

THU 2 – SAT 4 OCT · 2 NIGHTS · 3,500M ALTITUDE
Stay
Rima Yurt Camp. 4.6★. ~USD 45–60/night. Heated yurts (wood stove), shared toilets 50m away. Communal meals included. No WiFi (limited nights only).
Do (Core Experience)
Herding family immersion: Sunrise horse rides around lake (3–5hr). Participate in daily livestock work (watering, herding, meal prep). Work WITH families, not observation-only. Authentic nomadic life. Physically demanding, altitude fatigue real, deeply rewarding.
Eat
Bishbarmak (national dish, eaten by hand). Kumys breakfast. Hearty meals with herding families. Hydrate constantly (altitude).

Bishkek (Post-Trip)

SUN 5 – MON 6 OCT · 2 NIGHTS · 800M ALTITUDE
Stay
Freedom Hotel (same, familiar). 4.9★. Recover. Rest well before return flight.
Do (Choose 1)
Silk Road heritage: Burana Tower (11th-century minaret) + Tash Rabat caravanserai (historic inn, mountain setting). 6–7hr excursion, ~USD 50–80/group. OR Supara Chunkurchak gorge: 30min north, thermal springs, hiking, recovery day. OR light activity: Soviet monuments walk, artist galleries, Shoro Bazaar final wander (free, local-focused).
Eat
Recovery meals. Familiar spots. Evening flight prep.
