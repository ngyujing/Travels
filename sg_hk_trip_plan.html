03Basecamp & City Guide

Wan Chai HK Island strategy — everything within MTR reach

🏨 Dorsett Hong Kong GUESSING

387–397 Queen's Road East, Wan Chai, HK Island. ⚠ CRITICAL: There are multiple Dorsett properties in HK. Verify you're booking "Dorsett Hong Kong" (Wan Chai) and not "Dorsett Wanchai" or "Dorsett Kwun Tong" (Kowloon). Call to confirm address before paying deposit.

  • Rooms Mid-range: ~SGD 150–250/night. Confirm group room config (twin vs double) for 3–5 pax.
  • Location Wan Chai MTR ~5 min walk. Lockhart Rd hawker strip 8 min. Historic tram stop nearby (HKD 2.6 flat).
  • Airport Airport Express via Admiralty → Central (2 stops, 10 min). In-town check-in available at HK Station.
  • Hike Taxi/Uber to Dragon's Back trailhead ~HKD 120–150. Download HKTaxi or Uber app before departure.

🚇 Getting Around

  • MTR World-class metro. Octopus card covers MTR + buses + trams + Star Ferry + some convenience stores.
  • Tram Historic double-decker tram, HK Island east-west only. HKD 2.6 flat. Slow but scenic.
  • Ferry Star Ferry: Wan Chai or Central → TST Kowloon. HKD 3.4 (lower deck). Best value in HK.
  • Bus Critical Day 3: Bus 9 from Shau Kei Wan → Dragon's Back trailhead. Bus 6 → Stanley → Admiralty.

🌤 May Weather in HK

  • Temp 25–30°C, 80%+ humidity. Feels hotter than SG on the hills.
  • Rain LIKELY Start of rainy season. Pack a compact rain jacket. Afternoons most risky.
  • Hike Start Dragon's Back by 08:30. Afternoon thunderstorms possible May–Jun.
  • Views May = hazy. Peak views may be cloud-covered — still worth going for the tram experience.

💳 Money + Practical Info

  • Currency HKD. SGD 1 ≈ HKD 5.7–5.9 GUESSING — check rate before trip. HK is cheaper than expected once on the ground.
  • Octopus Buy at HKG airport. Load HKD 300 each. Works on almost everything. Refund unused balance at airport on departure.
  • Cash Keep HKD 300–500 cash daily for dai pai dongs, wet markets, and older hawker stalls that don't take cards.
  • SIM HK data SIM at airport: ~HKD 60 for 3-day unlimited. Beats roaming. WhatsApp works normally.
  • Plug Type G (UK 3-pin, same as SG). No adapter needed if you're from Singapore — standard SG plugs work in HK directly.

07Pre-Departure Checklist

Complete 1–2 weeks before departure

Accommodation & Logistics

  • 🔴 CRITICAL: Verify Hari Raya Haji 2027 date at mom.gov.sg before booking flights. Islamic holidays are moon-sighting dependent. If date shifts, trip efficiency drops.
  • 🔴 CRITICAL: Confirm exact Dorsett property — Dorsett Hong Kong (Wan Chai) ≠ Dorsett Wanchai ≠ Dorsett Kwun Tong. Call to verify address and room configuration for group size.
  • Book flights SIN → HKG → SIN. Target morning departure Day 1 (arrive HK by 13:00), afternoon/evening return Day 4.
  • Book Dorsett Hong Kong — confirm early check-in option or luggage storage for Day 1 arrival.
  • Book Peak Tram tickets online at thepeak.com.hk — weekend queues are brutal without pre-booking.
  • Check Tim Ho Wan reservation availability for Sat lunch (Day 2). Not all branches take bookings — call ahead.

Transport & Access

  • Download HKTaxi or Uber HK app on phone before departure. Useful for Day 3 Dragon's Back trailhead transfer.
  • Check Airport Express in-town check-in eligibility for your airline (SQ, CX, most majors qualify). Saves 30–45 min at HKG on Day 4.
  • Download Google Maps offline for HK — saves mobile data and works in MTR tunnels.
  • Check Bus 9 schedule from Shau Kei Wan to Dragon's Back trailhead (To Tei Wan Road). Confirm first bus time for Day 3.

Packing

  • 🔴 CRITICAL: Carry-on only for HK — Kota Tinggi Motocamping departs Thu 20 May. No time to repack large luggage between trips.
  • Trail runners or hiking shoes for Dragon's Back (Day 3). Exposed trail — no flip-flops.
  • Compact rain jacket / packable poncho — May is HK's rainy season onset. Afternoon storms likely.
  • Sunscreen SPF 50+, hat, sunglasses — Dragon's Back ridge is fully exposed.
  • Reusable 1.5L water bottle — refill at hotel. HK tap water is potable. Bring extra for the hike.
  • Small daypack (20L) for hiking day. Main bag stays at hotel.
  • Power bank — full days out from hotel. HK has fewer free-charging spots than SG.
  • No adapter needed from SG — HK uses UK Type G plugs, same as Singapore.

Communication

  • Buy HK data SIM at airport (~HKD 60, 3-day unlimited) or activate roaming before departure. SIM option is cheaper.
  • Create group WhatsApp/Telegram thread. Pin hotel address in both English and Chinese for taxi use.
  • Hotel address in Chinese for taxis: 香港灣仔皇后大道東387-397號 都匯香港 (Dorsett Hong Kong, Wan Chai)
  • Emergency: HK Police 999 · Tourist Helpline +852 2508 1234 · Singapore Emergency (from HK) +65 999
  • Save Typhoon Signal check source: HK Observatory app or hko.gov.hk — T8 or above = most activities cancelled.

Day-Before Checklist (Thu 13 May 2027)

  • Pack bags tonight — early airport departure Friday morning.
  • Check HK weather forecast for 14–17 May. Flag to group if typhoon signal is forecast — have backup indoor plan ready.
  • Confirm all group members have passports with 6+ months validity (expires after Nov 2027).
  • Confirm group meeting time/terminal at Changi. Allow 2.5h before departure.
  • Charge all devices and power banks. Download Octopus app for top-up convenience (optional).
  • Print or screenshot Dorsett booking confirmation + flight itinerary — don't rely on data at airport.

🧭 Travel DNA Updates

Hari Raya Haji as Efficiency Anchor (locked):

Islamic public holidays offer the highest leave efficiency potential (Monday PH = 4-day trip on 1 AL). However, moon-sighting dependency means the date is always GUESSING until the official MOM gazette, typically 1–2 months before. Build a ±1 day flight-date buffer or buy refundable fares until the date is gazetted.

HK Island Base Dominates Kowloon for Day-Trip Itineraries (locked):

When the itinerary is built around HK Island attractions (The Peak, Dragon's Back, Central, Soho, Stanley), a Wan Chai/Admiralty base eliminates harbour crossing friction. Kowloon night market is a Star Ferry excursion, not an anchor. Default to HK Island base for mixed food + hike + urban trips.

Back-to-Back Trip Protocol (locked):

Trips within 5 days of each other require explicit conflict flags in Section 00 and Day 4 notes. Packing rule: carry-on only for the shorter trip. Recovery buffer minimum = 3 days. Flag the conflict prominently; don't bury it in itinerary notes.
