Pre-Departure Checklist
Action Items Before You Book
🌀
Typhoon Season — Highest Risk Window
August is peak typhoon season in Hong Kong Certain. A Signal 8 or above means all outdoor attractions close, ferries halt, and most restaurants shut. A Signal 10 (rare) = full city shutdown. Most trips run fine — but if a typhoon hits during your 4 days, you will lose a day of activities with no recourse.

Action: Buy travel insurance with typhoon/weather disruption coverage before flying. Check HKO (Hong Kong Observatory) 48h before each day's activities: hko.gov.hk. Have an indoor backup plan for Day 3 (Harbour City, PMQ, Temple Street night market indoors).
🛂
Singapore Passport → HK Entry: 90-Day Visa-Free
Singapore passport holders currently enter Hong Kong visa-free for up to 90 days Likely. Verify with ICA or HK Immigration before travel — entry requirements can shift.
Action: Check immd.gov.hk ~3 months before departure.
✈️
6 Aug 2027 = Singapore National Day — Book Flights Early
Departing Singapore on National Day means high outbound demand and potential fare inflation Certain. Cathay Pacific and Singapore Airlines both fly direct SIN–HKG (~3h30m).
Action: Book flights 3–4 months ahead. Aim for a morning departure to maximise Day 1 in HKG.
🏨
CORRECTION: Base moved from TST (Kowloon) → Wan Chai (HK Island)
The original plan used Tsim Sha Tsui as basecamp. This version switches to Dorsett Hong Kong, HK Island. Changes: Day 1 evening now uses Wan Chai night food corridor instead of Temple Street (avoids a harbour crossing right after a long flight). Day 4 morning uses Tai Kwun instead of Jade Market. Kowloon night markets moved to optional. Days 2 and 3 are materially better from this base.

Note Dorsett operates multiple HK properties — verify you are booking Dorsett Hong Kong (not Dorsett Wanchai or Dorsett Kwun Tong). Confirm the exact address before booking.
🚇
Get an Octopus Card at the Airport
The Octopus card covers MTR, buses, ferries, 7-Eleven, and most restaurants Certain. Pick one up at the arrivals hall at HKG. Load HKD 200–300pp for 4 days. The Airport Express from HKG → Kowloon takes 22 minutes and drops you a 5-min walk from most TST hotels.

🔄
Indoor Backup Plan (Typhoon Signal 8+)
Replace Day 3 with: Harbour City mall (connected to hotel area) → IFC Mall → Art Basel at ArtHK if active → Dinner at any hotel restaurant (most reopen once signal lowers). Monitor HKO every 3 hours. Signals drop fast after a typhoon passes — sometimes a full afternoon opens up.
