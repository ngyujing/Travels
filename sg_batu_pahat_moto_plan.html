🌦️ Weather & Packing
  • November in Batu Pahat: Warm & humid, occasional rain (monsoon season). Expect 28–32°C.
  • Packing: Light clothes, sunscreen, helmet/riding gear, light rain jacket (optional), phone charger, cash (MYR).
  • Motorcycle gear: Helmets mandatory. Protective jacket/pants recommended. Bring spare gloves (hand grips get hot).
💰 Cash & Currency
  • Malaysia currency: MYR (Ringgit). Most venues accept cash; some cafes have e-payment (PayNow, Grab, Alipay).
  • Estimated spend: RM 200–250 per person (food + activities). Bring at least RM 300–400 per person.
  • ATMs available in Batu Pahat town center. Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank widely available.
