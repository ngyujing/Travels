05Dive Days — Kerama Islands

One hub, two visits (Days 3–4). Kerama National Marine Park, ~1hr boat from Tomari Port, Naha — no domestic flight, no second base. 4–6 dives total across 2 days sufficient for reef exploration and whale sightings.

Kerama Islands — Full-Day Boat Dive

TOMARI PORT DEPARTURE · ~7:30–8AM · 2–3 DIVES
Operators (named, verify current rates)
Aloha Divers Okinawa, SEAKERS Okinawa, or GOLD Scuba Diving — all English-capable, Naha-based, run daily Kerama boat trips with hotel pickup. Full equipment rental (BCD, regulator, 5mm wetsuit, fins, computer) runs around ¥4,000/day per diver at market rate.
Conditions in March
Water ~19–22°C, visibility often 20–30m+. This is winter/early-spring water — a proper wetsuit is non-negotiable, not a preference.
Bonus
Humpback whale season runs Jan 12–Mar 31 in the Kerama channel — several operators fold whale watching into the boat transit at no extra cost. Not guaranteed, weather- and luck-dependent.
Booking
Book both days as soon as your flights are confirmed — weekend slots (your Day 6 falls on a Thursday, lower risk, but book early regardless) fill with local and tour-group demand.
Certification note: CERTAIN these are fun dives requiring an existing open-water certification for the actual dive slots — if either of you isn't certified, operators offer "discover scuba" intro dives instead (shallower, instructor-led 1:1), which changes group logistics slightly. Confirm certification status for everyone before booking so the operator quotes the right package.

VisaCERTAIN Singapore passport holders get visa-free entry to Japan for short tourist stays (well within this trip's length). No action needed beyond a valid passport.

One source claims IDPs aren't valid for motorcycles in Japan at all; a specific Okinawa operator (okinawanorth.com) says 1949-Convention IDPs with Category A are accepted. Wildlife: Yanbaru Kuina (Okinawa Rail) — critically endangered flightless bird, endemic to Yanbaru, ~few thousand remain. Extremely shy; spotted dusk/dawn on forested roads (drive slowly). Early AM or night tours increase sighting chances. Wear proper hiking shoes — trails are muddy forest only.

Emi no Mise: Farm-to-table restaurant, serves "Choju-zen" (longevity meal) with 15+ local vegetables, ¥1,575 (reserve ahead: 0980-44-3220). Sangui (triangular rock tunnel) frames view of Kudaka Island ("Island of the Gods"). Entry ¥200. Stay campervan in Ogimi area.
South Okinawa (bike Day 8)Peace Memorial Park: Site of final WWII Battle of Okinawa (1945), ~22,000 soldiers & ~100,000 civilians died. Museum documents history. Cliff viewpoint over sea. Gyokusendo Cave: 5km limestone cave, 850m open to public walkways, spectacular stalactites/stalagmites formed over 300,000 years. Moist cave floor — sturdy shoes, not riding boots.

American Village: American-themed shopping/dining, sunset sea views. Ricoland: Motorcycle apparel shop, helmets, riding gear. Seaside Drive-In: Beachside dining with ocean views. All monorail-accessible or short taxi from Naha hotel.

Campervan: rest area parking is tight, arrive early for evening. Fuel at major stations. Traffic drives on left (same as Singapore). Watch for Yanbaru Kuina crossing roads at dusk.
