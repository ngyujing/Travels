03Dive Days — Kerama Islands

One hub, three visits. Kerama National Marine Park, ~1hr boat from Tomari Port, Naha — no domestic flight, no second base.

Kerama Islands — Full-Day Boat Dive

TOMARI PORT DEPARTURE · ~7:30–8AM · 2–3 DIVES
Operators (named, verify current rates)
Aloha Divers Okinawa, SEAKERS Okinawa, or GOLD Scuba Diving — all English-capable, Naha-based, run daily Kerama boat trips with hotel pickup. Full equipment rental (BCD, regulator, 5mm wetsuit, fins, computer) runs around ¥4,000/day per diver at market rate.
Conditions in March
Water ~19–22°C, visibility often 20–30m+. This is winter/early-spring water — a proper wetsuit is non-negotiable, not a preference.
Bonus
Humpback whale season runs Jan 12–Mar 31 in the Kerama channel — several operators fold whale watching into the boat transit at no extra cost. Not guaranteed, weather- and luck-dependent.
Booking
Book both days as soon as your flights are confirmed — weekend slots (your Day 6 falls on a Thursday, lower risk, but book early regardless) fill with local and tour-group demand.
Certification note: CERTAIN these are fun dives requiring an existing open-water certification for the actual dive slots — if either of you isn't certified, operators offer "discover scuba" intro dives instead (shallower, instructor-led 1:1), which changes group logistics slightly. Confirm certification status for everyone before booking so the operator quotes the right package.

06Practical Notes

TopicDetail
VisaCERTAIN Singapore passport holders get visa-free entry to Japan for short tourist stays (well within this trip's length). No action needed beyond a valid passport.
Flight schedule riskLIKELY Sun/Mon/Wed is the confirmed base pattern (per Scoot's own schedule filing), with Thursday added as a 4th weekly service from Apr 2026. Route is barely a year old at booking time — recheck the moment March 2027 schedules release, don't assume today's pattern is locked.
Motorcycle licence — the critical checkCERTAIN Your IDP must carry Category A (motorcycle), not just Category B (car). AA Singapore only issues this if your actual Singapore licence includes a motorcycle class — check this before applying, since it determines whether this whole plan is viable. One source claims IDPs aren't valid for motorcycles in Japan at all; a specific Okinawa operator (okinawanorth.com) says 1949-Convention IDPs with Category A are accepted. Confirm directly with your chosen rental shop before booking flights around this.
Bike rental shops (named, verify current rates)Sakihama Bike Shop (Naha, near the airport, "50cc to large vehicles") and rentalmotorbike.com (Honda/Kawasaki/Yamaha/Royal Enfield fleet) both serve Naha. Ask specifically for a 250–400cc model — you need expressway-legal capacity for Day 5's north run, not a scooter.
Getting around NahaWalk or Yui Rail (monorail) inside the city — don't bother riding the bike through Kokusai-dori traffic for short hops. One genuine motorcycle advantage: bikes are exempt from the vehicle restriction during Kokusai-dori's pedestrian-mall hours, so you can ride in when cars can't.
Getting around North Okinawa, Kouri/Manzamo & South OkinawaMotorcycle needed for Day 5, Day 7, and Day 8 specifically — public bus exists to each individually but is slow (North: 2hr+ with transfers) or doesn't connect the South Okinawa sites to each other directly (Peace Memorial Park and Okinawa World are separate bus lines with no link between them). Everywhere else in this itinerary, skip the bike — Naha itself is fully covered by monorail and walking.
Road conditions — what to actually watch forLIKELY Some Okinawa roads are limestone-paved and get slippery when wet — March is cooler/wetter than peak summer, so treat coastal corners with real caution after rain. Traffic drives on the left, same as Singapore — one less adjustment than most countries. Route 58 sees regular congestion; build in buffer time rather than cutting it fine on Day 5.
MoneyJapan still leans cash-heavy outside major chains. IC card (Suica-compatible) for the monorail; cash for markets and smaller eateries.
SIM/dataeSIM or airport SIM on arrival — needed for maps on the north-island and Kouri/Manzamo riding days, plus coordinating dive-day pickup times.
Weather — MarchLIKELY Okinawa in March is cool-mild (high teens to low 20s°C air), calmer than typhoon season, but still winter-pattern seas and occasional rain — relevant both to the dive-day risk flagged above and to riding gear (bring a rain layer for the bike).
EmergencyJapan emergency: 110 (police), 119 (ambulance/fire). DAN (Divers Alert Network) contact worth having on file given three dive days.
